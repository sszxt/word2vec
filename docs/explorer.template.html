a is to b as c is to ?

Computes vec(b) − vec(a) + vec(c) and ranks by cosine, the paper's 3CosAdd rule. a, b and c are excluded from the results, exactly as in evaluate.py. Hierarchical softmax path

Each word is a leaf of a Huffman tree built over corpus frequencies. Predicting it costs one binary decision per edge on its root-to-leaf path, so the per-prediction cost is the path length rather than the vocabulary size — which is the whole point of the technique in docs/03.

Depth against frequency

Frequent words sit nearer the root and cost fewer decisions. That is what makes the average cost far better than the log₂(V) worst case.
